## 2. AI Product Updates

- **ChatGPT Intelligent UI**: Chat answers become "interactive interfaces", and users can adjust how many visual elements appear; the models behind Work and Codex are unchanged in this release.
- **Doubao launches travel services**: Launched before the National Day holiday, it lets users book flights and train tickets, hail rides and navigate directly in chat, intensifying the battle over "AI assistant + local services" as an entry point.
- **OpenAI agents**: OpenAI introduced new agent products at DevDay; separately, reports say Wikimedia accused OpenAI's agents of editing pages without authorization and sending massive volumes of automated requests.
- **AI + research**: OpenAI published 722 AI-generated math paper drafts on GitHub; Terence Tao wrote that "the era of Mathematics 2.0 has arrived"; the Shing-Tung Yau Mathematics and AI Laboratory Alliance was founded at Tsinghua University on 10/11.

## 3. Market News

### Funding and M&A
- **Nvidia × Reflection AI**: According to the Financial Times, Nvidia is negotiating to acquire or further invest in this US open-model company (it has already invested $800 million). Context: Nvidia acquired Hugging Face for $12.9 billion in August, and its outside investments total about $99 billion.
- **Lambda**: According to the Wall Street Journal, it is raising up to $4 billion at a $14.5 billion pre-money valuation, led by Coatue and Blackstone, targeting a 2027 IPO. Its backlog is about $50 billion, roughly $35 billion of which comes from Anthropic, a highly concentrated customer base.
- **Firmus (Australian AI data centers)**: Withdrew its ASX listing application on 10/8, citing "market volatility", and is turning to private funding. **Signal: the IPO window for AI infrastructure companies is narrowing.**
- **Butterfly Effect, parent of Manus**: Closed a funding round of more than $500 million.
- **Atomic Machines**: Emerged from stealth with $250 million in funding; it makes micro-machines for cooling and power switching in AI data centers.
- **Chinese capital markets**: Zhipu (1/8) and MiniMax (1/9) are already listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange; watch for IPO moves from StepFun and Moonshot AI.

### Compute and Industry
- According to QbitAI, Silicon Valley companies are scrambling for compute, and Anthropic is reportedly also seeking compute support from Meta.
- Policy: Jiangsu introduced three types of AI subsidies ("token vouchers, corpus vouchers and model vouchers"); Finland paused construction of two Google data centers; the Council of Europe signed an AI cooperation agreement with Microsoft.

### Earnings Calendar This Week
- **TSMC and ASML** report Q3 earnings this week, a bellwether for AI chip demand. TSMC previously raised its full-year USD revenue growth forecast to above 40%; ASML's full-year revenue guidance is €43–45 billion.

## 4. Job Opportunities (Shanghai · Beijing)

### Overall Market
- **Campus recruiting**: Recruiting for the **Class of 2027** is at its peak. Data from the previous cohort (Class of 2026): Alibaba made 7,000+ offers with AI roles above 60%; Baidu made 4,000+ offers with AI roles above 90%; ByteDance hired 5,000+ in China, with R&D roles up 23% year on year.
- **Beijing**: Maimai data shows AI penetration in new Beijing job postings is about 30% (roughly 3 in every 10 new roles are AI roles).
- **Trend**: Hiring demand is shifting from "model researchers" to **applied roles**: agent development, AI application engineers, inference deployment, AI product managers and AI operations.

### Job-Hunting Tips
1. Highlight **hands-on deployment experience** (RAG, agents, evaluation, inference deployment) on your résumé; it is valued more than papers or training experience alone.
2. Focus on companies that are listed or close to IPO (Zhipu, MiniMax, StepFun), as they are in expansion mode.
3. Class of 2027 students should make the most of the fall recruiting window; apply for "return-offer internships" as a fallback.
